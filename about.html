Our Story

Craftsmanship rooted in tradition

Where Tradition Meets Timeless Design

Founded with a simple belief — that furniture should be built to last generations — Kiku Studio has been handcrafting premium hardwood furniture for years.

We work exclusively with Mvule and Mahogany, two of Africa's finest hardwoods. Each piece is shaped by master artisans who've spent decades perfecting their craft.
