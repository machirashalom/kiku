Furniture Made to Order

Kiku Studio builds beds, sofa sets and L-seats, dining sets, TV stands and coffee tables at our workshop in Nairobi. Each piece is made for the customer who orders it — to your size, your design and your finish.

Visit our showroom on Getathuru Road to see what we make, or message us on WhatsApp with the item you want and your town. We'll confirm the price and delivery quote before you order.
